# Research Framework

## Industry Boundary

- Define what is inside and outside the industry.
- Name adjacent industries and substitution risks.
- Identify whether the industry is policy-driven, technology-driven, demand-driven, or capital-cycle-driven.

## China 15th Five-Year Plan Position

- Explain why China treats the industry as strategically important.
- Connect the industry to national goals: industrial upgrading, self-reliance, security, aging population, productivity, energy transition, digital infrastructure, or future industries.
- Track central policy, local pilots, subsidies, procurement, standards, and regulatory constraints.
- Compare China's global position: strengths, weak links, import dependencies, export opportunities, and overseas competition.
- Identify which companies, regions, jobs, and investment themes are most exposed to policy support.

## Chain And Value Flow

- Map upstream inputs, core manufacturing or service layers, downstream customers, and end users.
- Mark where profit pools concentrate.
- Identify bottlenecks: supply, technology, regulation, channel, talent, data, compute, capital.

## Technology And Product

- Compare major technical routes.
- Mark maturity: lab, pilot, early commercialization, scaling, mature.
- Identify performance metrics and cost curves.
- Track standards, interoperability, and safety requirements.

## Market And Policy

- Record market size, growth, penetration, and geographic split.
- Separate total addressable market from actually serviceable near-term market.
- Track China policy support, procurement rules, subsidies, export controls, localization requirements, and safety regulation.

## Company Analysis

- Classify companies by chain position.
- Compare business model, customer type, revenue quality, margin structure, financing needs, and moat.
- For public companies, prefer filings and financial statements over media summaries.

## Investment View

- State thesis, catalyst, valuation anchor, downside case, and monitoring indicators.
- Explain what evidence would invalidate the thesis.
- Avoid recommendations without time horizon and risk framing.

## Career View

- Map role families: research, engineering, product, sales, clinical/regulatory, operations, investment, policy.
- Identify must-have skills, optional differentiators, and realistic entry projects.
- Use job postings as evidence, but separate hiring demand from marketing language.
