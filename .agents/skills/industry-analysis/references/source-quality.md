# Source Quality

## Preferred Source Order

1. Chinese national and local policy documents, 15th Five-Year Plan related materials, laws, standards, official statistics, regulatory databases.
2. Annual reports, quarterly reports, prospectuses, investor presentations, company announcements, exchange filings.
3. Peer-reviewed papers, preprints, technical documentation, patents.
4. Industry association reports, consulting reports, brokerage research.
5. Media interviews, conference notes, podcasts, hiring data.
6. Social posts, reposted charts, unsourced summaries.

## Claim Handling

- For factual claims, cite the exact source path or URL.
- For numerical claims, preserve unit, geography, period, and calculation method.
- For forecasts, record author, model assumptions, and date.
- For judgments, label them as judgments and list supporting and opposing evidence.

## Red Flags

- No original source for a chart.
- Market size without definition.
- CAGR without start and end year.
- Company ranking without metric.
- Policy interpretation without quoting or linking the policy.
- Technology claims without performance metric or operating condition.
- China policy claims without the original policy text, issuing body, date, and applicable geography.
