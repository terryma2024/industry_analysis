# GE Vernova Q3 2026

- **Third Quarter 2026 Highlights:**
	\- Orders of $29B, +96% organically led by equipment at Power and continued services strength
	\- Backlog <sup>1</sup> growth of $20B sequentially from equipment and services, reaching $197B in total
	\- Gas Power equipment backlog and slot reservation agreements grew from 116 to 122 GW; now anticipate reaching at least 130 GW by year-end 2026
	\- Revenue of $12.6B, +27%, +18% organically\* led by Power and Electrification
	\- Net income of $1.3B; net income margin of 10.2%
	\- Adjusted EBITDA\* of $1.9B; adjusted EBITDA margin\* of 15.4%, up +690 basis points organically\*
	\- Cash from operating activities of $6.1B; free cash flow\* of $5.4B
	\- $16.8B cash balance; $5.7B in capital returned to shareholders in the first nine months of 2026
	\- In October, the Board of Directors increased the share repurchase authorization to $20B, from $10B

**CAMBRIDGE, Mass., (October 28, 2026)** – GE Vernova Inc. (NYSE: GEV), a unique industry leader enabling the world to electrify to thrive and decarbonize, today reported financial results for the third quarter ending September 30, 2026.

“We delivered strong financial results in the third quarter as demand for electricity infrastructure increases around the world. We now expect our backlog to exceed $200 billion in the fourth quarter, with improved margins, and to exit 2026 with at least 130 GW of gas equipment under contract. Given our performance and confidence in our trajectory, we are raising our 2026 financial guidance,” said **GE Vernova CEO** [**Scott Strazik**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/scott-strazik/). “We continue to innovate and develop new solutions for utility and data center customers to meet growing demand, including electrification technologies that support integration and resiliency. Our opportunity to create value is accelerating and I’m proud of how our team is executing with discipline and focus.”

"We delivered another strong quarter with robust orders and revenue growth, margin expansion, and significant free cash flow generation. Our backlog continues to expand driven by equipment growth at Power and Electrification, at improved margins from favorable price and disciplined underwriting, and services growth at Power,” said **GE Vernova CFO** [**Ken Parks**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ken-s-parks/). “We ended the quarter with a cash balance of approximately $17 billion, up $4 billion sequentially, increasing our confidence in making high-growth, accretive investments and returning cash to shareholders through our dividend and increased share repurchase program. Based on our strong financial performance, we are raising our guidance for 2026 revenue, adjusted EBITDA margin, and free cash flow.”

##### About GE Vernova

GE Vernova Inc. (NYSE: GEV) is a purpose-built global energy company that includes Power, Electrification and Wind segments and is supported by its accelerator businesses. Building on over 130 years of experience tackling the world’s challenges, GE Vernova is uniquely positioned to help lead the energy transition by continuing to electrify the world while simultaneously working to decarbonize it. GE Vernova helps customers power economies and deliver electricity that is vital to health, safety, security, and improved quality of life. GE Vernova is headquartered in Cambridge, Massachusetts, U.S., with approximately 85,000 employees across approximately 100 countries around the world. Supported by the Company’s purpose, The Energy to Change the World, GE Vernova technology helps deliver a more affordable, reliable, sustainable, and secure energy future.
