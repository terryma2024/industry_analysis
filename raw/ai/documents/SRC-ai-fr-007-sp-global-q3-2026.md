S&P Global Reports Third Quarter Results; Announces Strategic Review for Capital IQ

and Associated Data Businesses

New York, NY, October 27, 2026 – S&P Global (NYSE: SPGI) today reported third quarter results. This earnings release and supplemental materials are available at http://investor.spglobal.com/Quarterly-Earnings.

The Company reported third-quarter 2026 revenue of $3.678 billion, an increase of 7% compared to the third quarter of 2025. Third quarter GAAP net income increased 20% to $1.298 billion and GAAP diluted earnings per share increased 26% to $4.45. Adjusted net income for the third quarter increased 4% to $1.337 billion and adjusted diluted earnings per share increased 9% to $4.58. Higher GAAP net income was driven by growth in operating profit, including the benefit from a gain on disposition in Energy. Higher adjusted net income was driven primarily by growth in adjusted operating profit, partially offset by higher taxes in the quarter.

The Company has initiated a strategic review of alternatives, including potential separation, for Capital IQ and associated data businesses, which constitute the majority of Kensho Data & Platforms within Market Intelligence. While the exact perimeter remains under review, the Company expects to retain RatingsDirect, RatingsXpress, and Enterprise Solutions. Capital IQ and the associated data businesses are well positioned to grow and thrive, and we believe separate ownership could provide dedicated investment and focus, positioning these businesses to accelerate innovation and growth.

Division results presented in this release also reflect a recast of the Company’s division financial information for all periods presented, including inter-segment adjustments and changes to expense allocation methodologies as outlined in our press release dated July 6, 2026.

Total revenue and organic constant currency revenue are now expected to be higher than the previous guidance ranges of 5.9% to 7.9% and 6.0% to 8.0%, respectively, both driven by higher expected revenue from the Ratings and Indices divisions. GAAP operating margin expansion is expected to be below the prior range of 335 to 360 basis points primarily due to adjustments to expected gain on dispositions, partially offset by higher revenue growth. Higher revenue growth and continued expense discipline are expected to drive adjusted operating margin expansion above the prior range of 35 to 60 basis points. GAAP diluted EPS is expected to be lower than the prior guidance range of $16.35 to $16.60 primarily due to adjustments to expected gain on dispositions, partially offset by higher revenue. Adjusted diluted EPS is expected to be higher than the prior guidance range of $17.50 to $17.75, driven by the combined impact of higher revenue and margin expansion. Non-GAAP adjusted guidance excludes amortization of intangibles related to acquisitions and acquisition and disposition-related costs.

There is no specific timeframe for the strategic review we announced, and there can be no assurance that the review will result in any transaction or particular outcome. As such, the guidance provided assumes a full contribution from Market Intelligence, as it is currently structured, for the full year.

As previously announced, the Board of Directors has authorized a quarterly cash dividend of $0.97 per share.
