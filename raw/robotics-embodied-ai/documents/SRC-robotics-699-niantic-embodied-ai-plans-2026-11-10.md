# Niantic embodied AI plans 2026-11-10

## The more of the world you simulate, the less each place costs.

[Explore Plans](https://www.nianticspatial.com/embodied-ai/plans#plans)

## Turning real places into training-ready simulations means balancing realism, accuracy, speed, and cost. Compare plans and find the right fit.

### Evaluate

3-month minimum

Measure the impact before making a longer commitment.

- 15 Places Library downloads across the term
- 15 capture minutes a month (~1 place)
- Additional minutes available
- Minutes roll over
- Training rights on every place
- GA models, standard training times
- Dedicated GCS bucket
- Standard support

### Train

Most teams start here

12-month minimum

Real-world diversity from day one, plus the pipeline to add the sites you deploy into.

- 100 Places Library downloads
- 60 capture minutes a month (~4 places)
- Additional minutes available at a discounted rate
- Minutes roll over
- Training rights on every place
- GA models, longer training times
- Dedicated GCS bucket
- Standard support

### Deploy

12-month minimum

For teams ramping up on customer sites, with QA and an SLA behind every batch.

- 300 Places Library downloads
- 120 capture minutes a month (~8 places)
- Captures excluded from training (negotiable)
- QA on every batch
- Delivery SLA and escalation
- Dedicated GCS bucket
- Priority support

### Enterprise

12-month minimum

Your world, your model. Built around your sites, your data terms and your volume.

- Uncapped Places Library downloads
- Access to and input on new places
- Negotiated capture volume
- Latest models, longest training times
- Enterprise API delivery
- Pipeline calibration by our R&D team
- Priority processing
- White-glove support

### Academic

Access real-world 3D data for academic research, teaching, and non-commercial projects.

- Apply for access to the Places Library for qualifying research projects.
- Explore paid licensing options for universities and academic departments.
- Access is subject to eligibility and intended use.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Which plan is right for my team?

**Evaluate** is designed to prove the impact on your training and evals before making a larger commitment. **Train** is the starting point for most teams that need real-world diversity and a pipeline for adding future sites. **Deploy** is for teams moving into production with customers and needing QA, delivery SLAs, and priority support. **Enterprise** is built around custom sites, data terms, volume, API delivery, and white-glove support.

Can we start with a small proof of concept?

Yes. **Evaluate** is a paid three-month plan that lets you validate performance before signing up for a larger plan. It includes 15 Places Library downloads across the term and 15 capture minutes per month.

How do the plans scale as we need more places and captures?

The allowances increase by plan. Evaluate includes 15 Places Library downloads and 15 capture minutes per month; Train includes 100 downloads and 60 capture minutes per month; Deploy includes 300 downloads and 120 capture minutes per month. Enterprise provides uncapped Places Library downloads and negotiated capture volume.

What happens if we need more capture time than our plan includes?

Both Evaluate and Train let you buy additional capture minutes, discounted on Train, and unused minutes roll over on both plans. As your needs grow, you can scale up custom-scene capture or move to a higher plan for larger allowances.

What level of support and delivery is included?

Evaluate and Train include standard support and a dedicated GCS bucket. Deploy adds QA on every batch, a delivery SLA with escalation, and priority support. Enterprise adds enterprise API delivery, pipeline calibration by Niantic Spatial's R&D team, priority processing, and white-glove support.
