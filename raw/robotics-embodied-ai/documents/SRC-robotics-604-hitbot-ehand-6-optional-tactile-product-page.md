# HITBOT eHand-6 optional tactile product page

![eHand-6](https://en.hitbot.cc/static/home/images/banner-product-info.jpg)

## Product features

#### Degree of freedom

Four-finger bending angle 60°+110°+170°, three joints of thumb flexible rotation (32°+44°+44°) accurately replicate human grasping, pinching, gripping and pushing actions.

#### Intelligent perception

Optional 5-channel tactile feedback sensor to build an all-round perception system and achieve 3N-10N dynamic force control.

#### Quick response

Complex gestures can be completed within 1.1s, quick and stable.

#### Payload

The gripping force can reach up to 10N and the pulling load capacity is up to 5kg.
