## Ready Out of the Box, Effortless Transitions.

Eliminates complex pre-setup, enabling effortless single-person operation. The foldable seated storage design allows compact packing and easy transport.

## A New Dimension of Dialogue, Naturally Human.

Powered by a self-developed voice interaction system, Oli supports natural bilingual (Chinese and English) conversation and voice-based control.
Custom knowledge bases can be uploaded to support diverse application scenarios.

## Born to Move, Designed to Perform.

Preloaded with 15 high-frequency interactive actions and dance routines for performance use. The dance library supports OTA updates, allowing easy access to the latest trending routines.
The dance library support OTA updates, while Studio software enables intuitive, no-code choreography.
