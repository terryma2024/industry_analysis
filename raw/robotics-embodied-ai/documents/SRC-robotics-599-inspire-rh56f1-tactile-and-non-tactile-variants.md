## RH56F1

The RH56F1 Series Dexterous Hand features an all-metal, integrated skeleton for superior rigidity. Itssoftware deeply fuses multi sensors with algorithms, enabling multi-mode control. Delivering 1kHzreal-time communication, 30kg passive payload, 15N fingertip force, and passing 20 stringent tests-it’s a better fit for robots!
