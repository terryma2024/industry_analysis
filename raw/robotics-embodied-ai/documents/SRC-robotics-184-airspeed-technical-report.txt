ABSTRACT
Embodied artificial intelligence development hinges on effective
data acquisition, yet it faces challenges such as high costs, limited
training scenarios, and a lack of standards for dataset. To overcome
these, we introduce AIRSPEED, an open-source platform for em-
bodied artificial intelligence data production. AIRSPEED features
hardware-software decoupling to reduce costs, supports multiple
devices for diverse data collection, interfaces with various sim-
ulation platforms for synthetic data generation, and automates
dataset construction. It comprises three services: data collection
for real-world data, data generation for synthetic data, and dataset
construction for organizing data pyramid structures. AIRSPEED
aims to enhance data production efficiency and quality, thereby
improving embodied artificial intelligence model performance. Our
experiment confirmed the effectiveness of AIRSPEED in improving
data production efficiency. All software are open-sourced and can
be found on this website: https URL.
