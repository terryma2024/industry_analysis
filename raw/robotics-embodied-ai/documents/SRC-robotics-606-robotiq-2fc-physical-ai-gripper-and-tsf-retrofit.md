# Robotiq 2FC physical AI gripper and TSF retrofit

## 2FC Physical AI Gripper

**Learn from contact-rich manipulation.  
Act with tactile intelligence.  
Improve through deployment.**  
  
A production-ready adaptive gripper with tactile sensing and embedded intelligence, helping robots learn from physical interaction and act reliably on what they learn, from the lab to production.

### Physical AI has a blind spot: touch

The industry is investing in vision, demonstrations and simulation, yet manipulation depends on touch. Most robots cannot feel what they grasp, so pressure, slip, force and position go uncaptured.  
  
Tactile sensing is not new; turning raw signal into training data and real-time behavior is the hard part. The 2FC captures contact-rich data, synchronized and labeled with physical events, and runs embedded intelligence on the gripper so robots act on what they feel.

### Contact-rich data

More modalities, timestamped and synchronized

- **Modalities:** pressure, vibration, proprioception, force, speed, position
- **Millisecond synchronization:** All signals are time stamped with millisecond-level precision within a shared time reference frame.
- **Unified API:** Access all data through a single framework

### Embedded Intelligence

With compute built into the gripper, the 2FC runs grasp skills locally and in real time:

- **Grasp quality assessment**:  
	"How confident am I in this grasp?"  
	- One confidence score the cell can act on.
- **Incipient slip detection**:  
	"The object is starting to slip."  
	- Automatic slip detection and labeling.
- **Object pose estimation:**  
	"Where is the object in my fingers?"  
	- State information for learning and downstream manipulation.
- **Object centering**:  
	"Move the robot to the right position."  
	- Closed-loop manipulation.
- **Advanced grip check**:  
	"Did I pick something, and is it the right thing?"  
	- Outcome verification and automatic episode labeling.

### Durability and Reliability

Train and deploy on the same hardware

- **Rated for 2M cycles:** One gripper from data collection to fleet deployment.
- **Compliance:** Enables your system to better react, recover, and adapt to uncertainty.
- **Mechanical intelligence:** Patented underactuated design for both encompassing and parallel grasps.
