# Digit 360 multimodal tactile sensor project

## Introducing Digit 360

#### A Meta FAIR Release

###### An artificial finger-shaped tactile sensor that digitizes touch with human-level precision. Equipped with over 18 unique sensing features, Digit 360 allows researchers to either combine its various sensing technologies or isolate individual signals for in-depth analysis of each modality. Digit 360 significantly surpasses previous sensors, detecting spatial details as fine as 7 microns and forces as small as 1 millinewton. Its rapid response time enables it to process touch information up to 30 times faster than humans, setting a new standard in tactile sensing technology.

[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.02479)

[GitHub](https://github.com/facebookresearch/digit360)

[Call for Proposals](https://digit.ml/cfp.html)

### What is Digit 360?

Digit 360 is a modular platform which provides rich, multi-modal digital touch-sensing capabilities through a hemispherical compliant embodiment. Digit 360 contains high-resolution sensors (~8.3 million taxels) that respond to omnidirectional touch, capture multi- modal signals, and use on-device artificial intelligence to process the data in real time.

### How do I use Digit 360?

Digit 360 is an advanced touch digitization platform, equiped with a USB-3.1 Type-C port. To control and stream data from Digit 360 the [drivers/interface are available here](https://github.com/facebookresearch/digit360).

### Can I buy a Digit?

We have partnered with [GelSight Inc.](https://gelsight.com/) to commercialize Digit 360.

For early access to Digit 360, you may apply to the [Call for Proposals](https://digit.ml/cfp.html).
